About 15-Minute Cities

A 15 minute city is one where all neccessary amenities can be reached within 15 minutes of walking or cycling

This tool allows you to visualize the 15-minute area around your location based on your selected travel method and speed.

It helps you understand how far you can travel via walking or cycling to reach essential amenities like hospitals, schools, parks, and more.

How to use this tool

To use this tool, select your travel method, speed, and the amenity you want to find by using the menu on the left hand side of the screen.

Then click on the map to set your location. If you do not select a location the default is Exeter City Centre.

The map will then display the 15-minute area around your location, pinpointing amenities of the type selected.

Comparing two areas

You can also compare two areas by selecting the "Compare Areas" option from the menu.

In the comparison mode, you can set two different locations and see how far you can travel to reach the selected amenities from both locations.

This feature is useful for understanding the accessibility of amenities in different parts of the city.

About the Data

The data used in this tool is sourced from OpenStreetMap.

It includes various amenities such as hospitals, schools, parks, and more.
